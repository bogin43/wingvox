    How it works

    Everything runs on your machine

    HoldPress your hotkey and start talking.
    HearSpeech becomes text, on your own device.
    TidyA local model strips the ums and fixes punctuation.
    PasteThe text lands wherever your cursor is.

    Not just English

    100 languages, one command

    Wingvox dictates in English by default. Switch with one command from the Wingvox folder: ./set-language.sh fr. Flemish has no separate code of its own — it transcribes as Dutch (nl), the same written standard as Netherlands Dutch.
